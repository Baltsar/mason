# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). The short form is below; the full text at that address is what holds.

## The pledge

Everyone who takes part here, in issues, pull requests, the collection of workflows and anywhere else the project is talked about, is treated with respect, whatever their age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, level of experience, nationality, appearance, religion or sexual orientation.

## What that means

Wanted:

- Being kind and patient, also with a first question.
- Giving and taking criticism about the work, not the person.
- Owning a mistake, saying so, and mending it.

Not accepted:

- Insults, mockery, and personal or political attacks.
- Harassment, in public or in private.
- Sexual language or advances of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without their clear permission.

## A workflow you share is public

A file in the collection carries the name its owner gave it. Share only your own, and read every rule in it before you send it.

## If something is wrong

Write to the maintainer, Gustaf Garnow, at the address on [his GitHub profile](https://github.com/Baltsar). What you write is kept between you and him.

He may remove, edit or refuse comments, commits, issues and workflows that break this, and may keep someone out of the project, for a time or for good. He will say why.
